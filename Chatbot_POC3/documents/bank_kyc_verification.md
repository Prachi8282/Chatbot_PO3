# Know-Your-Customer (KYC) Verification Procedures

## 1. Customer Identification Program (CIP)
In accordance with federal regulations and anti-money laundering (AML) laws, we must verify the identity of every customer who opens a new account. The following core information must be collected prior to account activation:
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Physical residential address (P.O. Boxes are not accepted)
- Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) for US persons, or passport number for foreign nationals.

## 2. Acceptable Identity Documents
To verify the identity information, applicants must provide two forms of documentation:
1. **Primary Identification (Must contain a photograph)**:
   - Unexpired US Passport or foreign passport
   - State-issued Driver's License or ID card
   - US Military ID
2. **Secondary Identification (Must prove address)**:
   - Utility bill (electric, water, gas, or internet) dated within the last 60 days
   - Recent bank statement from a major financial institution
   - Unexpired residential lease agreement

## 3. Customer Risk Scoring and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
During the onboarding process, customers are assigned a risk score (Low, Medium, High) based on their occupation, country of origin, source of wealth, and expected transaction volumes. High-risk customers—such as Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) or customers from countries on the FATF warning list—must undergo Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). EDD requires:
- Verification of the source of funds (tax returns, business financials).
- Semi-annual review of account activity.
- Approval from the Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) prior to onboarding.

## 4. Periodic Review and KYC Refresh
KYC documentation must be periodically updated to ensure accuracy. Low-risk accounts are refreshed every 5 years. Medium-risk accounts are refreshed every 3 years. High-risk accounts are refreshed annually. Failure to provide updated documents upon request within 30 days will result in restriction of outgoing wire transfers and check cashing privileges.
